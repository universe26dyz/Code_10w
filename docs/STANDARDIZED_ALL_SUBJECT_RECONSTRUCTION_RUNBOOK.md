# Standardized all-subject reconstruction runbook

After successful standardization/reuse audit, every subject requires fresh Proposed reconstruction, Fit-first T1/T2 reconstruction, optimized poses, central/psf128 exports, native bundles, support masks, metrics, figures, and summary. Old 3D reconstructions and derived artifacts are never reused. Dictionary maps may be reused only after per-group content and geometry checks; frozen decoder/B6 may be reused only after SHA256 validation.
